Heal

Watch on youtube

Heal is a Social Interaction Design project inspired by confession chambers in churches. It offers an anonymous platform for users to share their concerns and listen to others, fostering genuine connections and providing emotional relief. The goal is to create a safe, non-judgmental space for people to connect and find support in times of distress.
