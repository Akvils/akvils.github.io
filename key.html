Key

With Elysian Studio

As the director of 'Key,' a VR experience, I oversaw a team of 5 to the Himalayas, where we lived in and closely observed the daily lives of young monks at Key Monastery. Drawing from this immersive experience, we crafted a VR journey that transports users to the serene world of the Buddhist hill monastery. 'Key' has earned international recognition, winning prestigious awards for its portrayal of monk life and the monastery's beauty.
