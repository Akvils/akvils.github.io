Paritta App

Landing page
github repo

I developed a Flutter app dedicated to Parittas, Buddhist Suttas known for providing protection from dangers and calamities to those who chant or listen to them. This app serves as an audio-visual book for these protective verses, merging ancient scriptures with multimedia elements. My role involved in-depth scripture examination and collaboration with monks to bring this sacred text into the digital realm.
